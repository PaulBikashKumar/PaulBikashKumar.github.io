About me

AI for biology.
Research for better health.

I’m Bikash Kumar Paul, a PhD student in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, also pursuing an MS in Computer Science and Applications (2024–2026), working at the intersection of machine learning, biomedical data, and precision medicine.

My research interests span multimodal learning, multi-omics integration, spatial transcriptomics, medical imaging, and electronic health records. I am interested in how interpretable and generative models can help us understand biological variation and investigate clinically relevant questions.

Before my doctoral studies, I taught and mentored students in Bangladesh at Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University, Daffodil International University, and Ranada Prasad Saha University. At Virginia Tech, I serve as a Graduate Research Assistant and Graduate Teaching Assistant. I have been an IEEE member since 2015.
